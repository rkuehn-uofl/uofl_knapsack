About
The University of Louisville Digital Collections is a growing resource which includes rare and unique images, documents, and other materials from our archives and special collections.

Collections are selected for this website by their curators and evaluated by site administrators for their access, preservation, and other needs or opportunities enhanced or presented by the creation of digital surrogates and standardized information about them.

Following our migration to this new open source platform (Samvera Hyku), some previously-available materials have been removed and relocated to other platforms, while others have not yet been reuploaded.

Please contact us with questions and comments.

Harmful Image and Language Statement
Please be advised that these materials contain images and language that may be upsetting and difficult to view. The documents, images, and publications presented here have been preserved and unaltered in order to present the materials in their original state and context. The content of these materials do not reflect the values of the University of Louisville or Archives and Special Collections.
